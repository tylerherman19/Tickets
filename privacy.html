Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Ticketline is a small personal project that watches ticket prices and emails you when they hit your budget. It collects only what it needs to do that, does not sell or share your data for advertising, and uses no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. Administrators can review account, alert, notification and operational activity already stored to operate the service. We do not collect page views or anonymous session analytics.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign in. It is used to send sign-in links, alert confirmations and price alerts.
  • Your alerts: the event, team or date you follow, ticket quantity, price limit, seating preference, alert name and whether alerts are paused, archived or marked purchased (with an optional price paid).
  • An optional cell number and carrier, only if you turn on text alerts. It is stored in a private table, used only to build your carrier’s email-to-text address, and is hidden from ordinary account pages after you save it. Authorized administrators can review delivery destinations in the protected admin dashboard.
  • Search activity: submitted search terms and opened search links, filters, time, visible first-page result count and errors. Administrators use this to understand demand and missing coverage. Signed-in searches are linked to your account; signed-out searches are anonymous and unlinked to one another. We do not store IP addresses or visitor identifiers in search activity. Common email and long number patterns in terms are removed, but please do not enter personal information in search.
  • Alert history: prices we checked for your alerts and a log of each notification attempt (time, channel and whether the mail server accepted it).

Browsing events without signing in requires no account. When you search, we may request a fresh price check for the events shown; that request contains only the event ID.

What we don’t collect

No third-party analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting or persistent visitor identifiers. No payment details: you buy tickets directly from the marketplace. We do not set cookies. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser’s local storage and removed when you sign out.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in service.
  • Google (Gmail) sends alert emails, and your carrier’s email-to-text gateway delivers optional texts.
  • GitHub hosts this website (GitHub Pages) and runs the scheduled price checks (GitHub Actions).
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy, including standard server logs such as IP addresses. Price data comes from Gametime’s public pages. We never send your personal information to Gametime or any other ticket marketplace; “View tickets” and “Compare” links simply open their sites.

How long we keep it

Your account, alerts and their history are kept until you delete them. Archiving an alert stops it but keeps its history so you can restore it. Deleting your account removes your sign-in, every alert, all price and delivery history, and any saved phone number. A minimal change log (alert ID, action and time, with no email or phone number) is retained for security. Public event and price data is not personal and is removed after events pass.

Search activity is removed after 90 days by scheduled cleanup and when new activity is recorded or reviewed. Deleting your account also removes searches linked to it. Anonymous searches cannot be linked back to a visitor for individual deletion.

Your choices

  • Turn off text alerts on any alert at any time from My alerts.
  • Pause or archive alerts from My alerts.
  • Delete your account and data yourself from Notifications. It takes effect immediately.
  • Sign out to remove your session from this browser.

Price history and retention

Signed-in searches and refreshes can save public marketplace prices and listing details for future comparisons, without enabling email or text alerts. Opening an event while signed in tracks it, so its price keeps being checked and saved; we keep your 200 most recently opened events and drop older ones. Personal history tracking is visible to your account and authorized administrators. Public price observations do not identify who requested them. Passed-event prices, seats, related alert logs and tracking entries are deleted after the full event date has passed; your alert definitions and notification settings remain until you delete them. Request-budget logs retain only operational IDs for two days.

Security

Your alerts are protected by row-level security. Your signed-in account and explicitly authorized Ticketline administrators can access your alert records. Administrators can review account email addresses, notification destinations, alert settings, usage and operational records to operate the service. Administrator access and changes are audited. Authentication secrets and tokens are excluded from the admin dashboard. All traffic uses HTTPS.

Children

Ticketline is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. For questions, open an issue on the project’s GitHub page. Issues are public, so please don’t include your email, phone number or other personal details there; use in-app deletion for anything about your own data.
